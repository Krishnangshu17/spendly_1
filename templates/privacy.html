Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we also store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so the Service can display it back to you.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in your dashboard. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in Spendly's database for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your associated data will be removed within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and font services) to operate the Service. These providers process data only to the extent necessary to provide their service to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
